Business strategy · Infrastructure finance · October 2026

Renewable infrastructure investment strategy

Engineering Business Futures Case Competition · Case provided by Connor, Clark & Lunn Infrastructure (CC&L). Our three-person team evaluated ways to improve the long-term value of an illustrative portfolio of five Ontario solar farms and five British Columbia hydro plants.

01 / The challenge

Choose which operational upgrades and investment options could recover lost production, control operating costs and create long-term value—without overlooking capital requirements, contract restrictions and execution risk.

02 / My contribution

I researched the solar and hydro markets, evaluated possible operating improvements, helped develop phased investment recommendations and their timing, and contributed to communicating the recommendation in our team presentation. I considered the team's financial comparisons when prioritizing options; I was not the primary developer of the financial models.

03 / Approach

Market and industry research; strategic alternatives; technical and commercial feasibility; capital costs; operating costs; incremental revenue; NPV and payback comparisons; prioritized pilot-and-scale roadmap; presentation and Q&A.

04 / Recommendation

Our submitted team deck favored an approximately $7M digital-twin and targeted-repair program across ten assets, with a projected $17–29M NPV under its modeled assumptions. We proposed piloting before fleet-wide rollout, testing panel replacement separately, and deferring battery investment unless its return cleared a defined hurdle.

How we compared the options

Financial lens

We considered upfront capital, expected cash-flow uplift, operating costs, NPV, payback and sensitivity to assumptions. Our larger team recommendation used a different, more developed scenario than the earlier comparison slide; the two sets of numbers should not be mixed.

Strategy & delivery lens

We assessed technical maturity, regulatory constraints, implementation effort, scalability and risk. The proposed roadmap prioritized lower-risk repairs and analytics first, followed by measured pilot results and decision gates before major reinvestment.

Outcome & reflection

The team delivered an investment recommendation and presentation for a time-limited university case competition. For me, the key experience was bringing together market evidence, practical operating constraints and financial trade-offs to recommend what to do first, what to test, and what to defer. The stated NPV figures are modeled projections, not investment results.
