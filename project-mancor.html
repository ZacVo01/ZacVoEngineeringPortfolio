Manufacturing

Trunnion welding cell improvement

Adapt a trunnion welding production cell to lower demand while using less floor space and maintaining productive flow.

01 / Problem statement

Adapt a trunnion welding production cell to lower demand while using less floor space and maintaining productive flow.

02 / My contribution

Redesigned the manufacturing layout, supported tooling/fixture work, completed time studies and documented standard work for welding and shot-blast processes.

03 / Engineering methods and tools

Lean manufacturing, process mapping, SolidWorks, AutoCAD, time studies, standard work

04 / Results and outcomes

The redesigned cell reduced floor-space requirements by approximately 30%. Reported cycle time changed from 2 h 30 min to 1 h 55 min.
