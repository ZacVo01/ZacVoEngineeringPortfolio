Four-wheel omni-directional rover | MIE444

Integrate movement, sensing, communication and control for a student-built omni-wheel mobile robot.

01 / Problem statement

Integrate movement, sensing, communication and control for a student-built omni-wheel mobile robot.

02 / My contribution

Worked with Arduino Mega 2560 control, L298N motor drivers, motor encoders, Bluetooth, sensor integration and power troubleshooting as part of a team.

03 / Engineering methods and tools

Arduino, KiCad, embedded C++, motor drivers, PWM, I²C, UART, prototyping, troubleshooting

04 / Results and outcomes

Built and tested rover subsystems. Load-related motor and Bluetooth reliability challenges were observed; fully verified autonomous operation is not claimed.
