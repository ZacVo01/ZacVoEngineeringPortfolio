Can-opener redesign

Study a manual can opener and improve the torque transmitted by its gear mechanism.

01 / Problem statement

Study a manual can opener and improve the torque transmitted by its gear mechanism.

02 / My contribution

Modeled the original and modified mechanisms, redesigned gears and used mechanical mates to examine motion.

03 / Engineering methods and tools

SolidWorks, mechanical design, gearing, assembly motion

04 / Results and outcomes

The original project reports a 50% increase in output torque for the redesign; supporting calculations are not included in the public repository.
